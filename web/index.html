The painting

Pieter II Brueghel’s Village Lawyer (1621) supplied the room: petitioners, produce, anxious faces and more paper than anyone can manage. Here, its smallest occupant becomes the advocate.

The painting belongs to MSK Ghent. The public-domain reproduction is served with this app. The petitions and village charter are original fiction, not a reconstruction of Flemish law.

The experiment

I built this to ask what happens when measured animal wiring is given an unfamiliar job. Structured facts stimulate sensory neurons. Activity propagates through a numerical model. A small artificial readout learns to associate that activity with one of eight pieces of advice.

The wording of the advice is a fixed template, not the fly explaining its thoughts. A confident-looking answer may still be wrong. The test results remain visible, including when an ordinary classifier does better.

What “complete” means here

The source is Janelia FlyEM MaleCNS v1.0, licensed CC BY 4.0. The app retains all 166,700 neurons with a source superclass annotation, and all 25,582,938 published neuron-pair edges between them. It retains 217 isolated neurons too.

The release also contains unclassified segments and glia. Those are not silently counted as simulated neurons. Source synapse confidence is at least 0.5; there is no additional app-side edge cutoff. Every downloaded source and every packaged graph chunk is checksum-verified.

What is approximated

This is a 24-step leaky-tanh rate reservoir. Incoming connection counts are normalized. GABA and glutamate outputs receive a negative sign; other or unknown transmitters receive a positive sign. That simplification is not a receptor-level model. Membrane dynamics, gap junctions, neuromodulation and a physical fly body are not reconstructed.

Teaching changes an artificial ridge-regression readout. The original biological edges do not learn. There is no claim that the system is conscious, understands a petition or can provide legal advice.

Your data

All simulation and teaching run in a browser worker. There is no account, analytics, language-model API or server-side case processing. Nothing is saved automatically. Exported casebooks include your text; model exports contain numerical training states. Only use fictional examples.
