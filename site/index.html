An apprenticeship in eight answers

The teaching set contains 64 invented fact sheets. A small artificial classifier learns which advice to associate with the fly network’s activity. The biological wiring stays fixed.

The fly is not reading a law book. Each confirmed fact is converted into stimulation of annotated sensory neurons.

No learned model in this session.

The chamber’s fictional customs

  1. Refer grave threats to the magistrate.
  2. Without independent evidence, seek a witness or a sworn account.
  3. For clearly identified goods, request their return.
  4. For an evidenced, due bargain, request payment.
  5. Seek compensation for substantial loss; propose settlement for smaller losses and boundaries.
  6. Otherwise, recommend not pursuing the claim.

Rules are applied in this order. They are deliberately simplified game rules, not historical findings or legal advice.

All 166,700 superclass-annotated neurons and all 25,582,938 directed connections between them are retained. The upstream confidence filter is 0.5. Unannotated segmentation fragments are not treated as neurons.

The browser uses all retained connections at every recurrent step. No extra edge threshold, sensory-edge deletion, crop, or weight quantization is applied.

The parts I invented

I map the nine fact fields onto sensory stimulation. Twelve steps of signed, leaky-tanh rate dynamics turn that stimulation into activity. These mathematical units are not a validated emulation of the animal’s physiology.

A 128-dimensional signed summary of non-input activity feeds a trained linear classifier. Directly stimulated neurons are excluded from that summary. Learning changes the classifier, not the connectome.

The eight recommendations, written explanations, villagers, and consequences are authored. There is no language model, no real legal practice, and no evidence that a fly’s consciousness has been uploaded. The small fly on the painting is an illustration, not a motor-neuron readout.
